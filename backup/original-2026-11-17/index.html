Common questions

Will it hurt?

For most treatments you should feel pressure but not pain. We numb the area fully first and check with you before starting.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, every day till 8 pm. Booked patients are seen first, so a quick call ahead saves you waiting.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Plans above ₹20,000 can be split over 3, 6 or 12 months at 0% interest.

Is the consultation fee adjusted?

If you go ahead with treatment within 30 days, the ₹500 consultation fee is taken off your bill.

How often should I come in?

Every six months for a check-up and clean suits most adults. We will tell you if you can leave it longer.
